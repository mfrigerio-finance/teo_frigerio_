# Executive Finance & Capital

## Profile

Executive finance work spanning strategic finance, CFO-office support, accounting, FP&A, treasury, capital allocation, valuation, risk and management reporting.

The emphasis is on connecting forward-looking decisions with controlled financial evidence: budgets and scenarios should reconcile to operational drivers, accounting outputs should remain explainable, and capital decisions should make assumptions and risk explicit.

## Strategic Finance & CFO Office

- Finance business partnering and commercial decision support
- Planning, performance management and executive reporting
- Capital planning and allocation
- Investment appraisal and scenario analysis
- Executive and board-level stakeholder communication
- Cross-functional coordination across finance, technology, operations, suppliers and advisers

## FP&A & Controlling

- Budgets and rolling forecasts
- Driver-based planning
- Variance analysis
- Profitability, margins and cost-driver analysis
- KPI frameworks and executive dashboards
- CAPEX / OPEX analysis
- Cost allocation, product costing and project economics

## Accounting & Group Finance

- Swiss and international accounting environments
- GL, AP/AR, accruals, fixed assets and reconciliations
- Month-end, quarter-end and year-end close
- IFRS and Swiss GAAP FER
- Consolidation, reporting packages and intercompany
- Eliminations and currency translation
- Statutory accounts, VAT and audit support
- Internal controls, audit trails and evidence packs

## International Accounting Coverage

Professional materials include accounting and reporting coverage across:

- **Switzerland** — Swiss GAAP FER, Swiss Code of Obligations / statutory reporting
- **USA / Canada** — US GAAP, SOX, Canadian IFRS
- **UK / Europe** — UK GAAP, IFRS, EU statutory reporting and VAT
- **UAE / GCC** — IFRS, UAE VAT and wider GCC operating context
- **Singapore / Hong Kong** — SFRS(I), HKFRS and statutory reporting
- **Australia / Japan** — AASB and Japanese GAAP
- **International groups** — consolidation, intercompany and audit support

## Operating principle

A finance output should answer more than “what happened?”. It should expose the economic driver, the accounting/control implication, the available decision and the person who owns that decision.
