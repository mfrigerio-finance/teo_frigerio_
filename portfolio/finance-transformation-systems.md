# Finance Transformation & Enterprise Systems

## Positioning

Finance transformation is treated as a chain linking policy, process, master data, system configuration, integrations, controls, reporting and user behaviour.

The objective is not simply to introduce a new platform. It is to make sure the finance logic remains coherent from business requirement through implementation, UAT, adoption and operational control.

## Transformation Scope

- Finance operating models and target-state design
- As-is / to-be process mapping
- Process redesign and standardisation
- Requirements gathering and functional specifications
- Acceptance criteria and UAT
- Defect analysis and remediation
- Integration and data-flow design
- Controls, segregation of duties and auditability
- Documentation, training and adoption
- Continuous improvement based on exceptions, root causes and cycle times

## Delivery Model

1. **Discover** — understand objectives, pain points, stakeholders, controls, data ownership and reporting needs.
2. **Define** — translate business requirements into target processes, system requirements and acceptance criteria.
3. **Design** — align finance logic, data structures, roles, integrations and controls.
4. **Validate** — reconcile outputs, execute UAT and test representative scenarios.
5. **Adopt** — document decisions, support users and manage transition dependencies.
6. **Improve** — analyse exceptions, defects, cycle times and root causes.

## Control-by-Design

The transformation model explicitly carries through:

- Segregation of duties
- Approvals
- Reconciliations
- Audit trails
- Change control
- Data ownership
- Traceability

## Why the Finance + Technology Combination Matters

Technical fluency improves finance delivery because requirements become more precise, integrations become more testable, automation becomes safer and communication with engineering teams becomes more effective.

The goal is a finance process that is not only functional, but explainable, controlled and operable after the transformation team leaves.
